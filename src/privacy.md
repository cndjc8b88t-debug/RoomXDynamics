# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 25, 2026*

RoomXDynamics is a room climate simulation for iPhone. This page explains what happens with your data in the app and on this website (roomxdynamics.com). They are handled separately below. The German and English versions have the same content and are equally valid.

---

## The app

The RoomXDynamics app does not collect personal data. It has no user account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party software development kits. All calculations run on your iPhone; nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not save your inputs.

If you have allowed it in your iPhone settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage data with app developers. These reports do not identify you to us. We use them only to find and fix errors. Apple's handling of this data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

The privacy information on the App Store says the same: no data collected.

---

## This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no embedded content from third parties. The site uses your device's built-in fonts; nothing is loaded from a third-party font service. There is no form that sends data to a server. A small script on the home page pauses the clip when you tap “Pause” or scroll it out of view, and lets it play again; it stores nothing and sends nothing.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, the host's servers automatically process technical data your browser sends, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and your browser type, in server log files. This is necessary to deliver the site and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website.

GitHub may process this data in the United States. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). How long GitHub keeps these logs is governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement); we have no access to them.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer you, and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed for that purpose, unless statutory retention periods apply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

## Former waitlist

We no longer run a waitlist. If you joined it by email before November 2026, we keep your email address only to send you one notification when the app is released. We delete the emails and addresses within 30 days after that notification, and no later than 12 months after we received them. If you withdraw your consent before then, by email to {{ site.contactEmail }} or {{ site.privacyEmail }}, we delete them immediately.

- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you sent the email.
- **Recipients:** The emails are stored in a mailbox at iCloud Mail, a service of Apple. For customers in the EU, Apple's contracting party is Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland. Apple may also process data in the United States. According to Apple, transfers of personal data from the EU are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). We do not pass the data on to anyone and do not copy addresses into other systems.

## Your rights under the GDPR

For the data described under "This website" and "Former waitlist", and for emails you send us, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw your consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR). To use them, write to the contact address below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Düsseldorf, Germany. If you live in the United Kingdom, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
